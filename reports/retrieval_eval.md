## Method

- P@k: for each query with expected ids, the share of the top-k ids that are expected; a missing result counts as a miss. Averaged over answerable queries. MRR and recall@5 use the top 5.
- Rejection accuracy: share of queries with no expected ids that got no result. False rejections: answerable queries that got none.
- Grid: {'w_dense': [0.3, 0.5, 0.7], 'g_member': [0.5, 0.7, 1.0], 'lex_k': [2.0, 5.0, 10.0]}; TAU candidates: quantiles [0.05, 0.15, 0.25, 0.35, 0.45, 0.55, 0.65, 0.75, 0.85, 0.95] of the TRAINING queries' best scores.
- Objective: mean P@5 (answerable) + rejection accuracy (unanswerable). Ties: higher objective, then P@3, then the median tied TAU, then grid order.
- Fixed (not tuned): {'assign_min': 0.3, 'noise_df': 0.15}.
